Featured project

Japan Shopping Assistant

A Chrome extension for international shoppers that shows estimated Japanese Yen prices for Mercari Japan listings and eBay search results, including estimated shipping on eBay. It can also apply eBay's “Located in Japan” filter, with each feature controlled independently from the popup.
